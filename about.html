About CTI.AC.PK

Making recruitment transparent, fast, and effortless for students and educators across Punjab.

Our Mission

At CTI.AC.PK, our core mission is simple: to make things easy for students and College Teaching Interns (CTIs). Navigating government recruitment schedules, calculating precise academic merits, and keeping track of dynamic vacancy announcements can often be confusing. We built this platform to bridge that gap and provide a centralized, frictionless hub for everyone participating in the Punjab Higher Education Department's CTI inductions for 2026 and 2027.

What We Provide for CTI 2026 & 2027

We provide every essential resource required for a successful CTI application journey under one roof:

Merit Calculators

Accurate calculation tools structured according to official 100-mark merit formulas (academic breakdowns, interviews, and higher qualifications).

Live Community Discussions

Real-time candidate chat rooms and discussion spaces equipped with intelligent unread reply tracking to help applicants clear doubts instantly.

Document Tools

Free, fast web utilities like our specialized Image Compressor optimized to prepare clean application documents under strict size limits.

Direct Updates & Guidance

Up-to-date procedural guides, application instructions, and policy breakdowns for new and experienced applicants across Punjab.

Independent & Transparent

CTI.AC.PK operates as an independent public guidance and utility portal. Our objective is to support candidates by organizing public data into easy-to-use digital formats. To sustain free access to our calculators and tools without paywalls, the site displays non-intrusive advertisements managed by trusted third-party networks like Google AdSense.
