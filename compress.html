1. Take a Clear Photo
Place your DMC, Domicile, or CNIC under good lighting. Crop out any unnecessary background edges.

Log in to cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk, go to your profile section, and upload the saved file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my upload failing with a "500 Server Error"? The HED portal frequently throws server errors when candidates attempt to upload original photos taken directly from high-resolution smartphone cameras (which are typically 3MB to 8MB). Compressing the image under 200KB fixes this issue.
Will compressing my DMC make the text illegible? No. Our tool uses smart bilinear canvas resizing, keeping the text sharp and readable while stripping out heavy background camera metadata.
Is it safe to compress sensitive documents like CNIC here? Yes, 100%. The compression logic runs completely inside your browser's memory using JavaScript. No files are transmitted to any server.
