The Guide to CTI Induction 2026–2027

سی ٹی آئی بھرتی کی مکمل تفصیلات اور قواعد

1
Introduction to the CTI Induction Scheme

The Higher Education Department (HED), Government of the Punjab, conducts the College Teaching Interns (CTI) program to resolve acute teaching staff shortages across government associate and graduate colleges in the province. The prime objective is to ensure that intermediate and undergraduate degree classes proceed without academic interruptions while regular PPSC recruitment processes are finalized. Hiring is strictly temporary as a stop-gap measure.

For the 2026–2027 session, the application framework uses a centralized digital portal (cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk) to manage profile registration and eliminate physical crowding. Placements are offered for an academic session or until regular faculty arrives, whichever occurs earlier.

2
Phase 1 & Phase 2 Application Procedure

To maintain transparency, the induction is separated into two operational phases:

  • Phase 1 (Active Now): Candidates must create an account on cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk using their CNIC. Applicants record personal details, academic marks (Matriculation through Master's/BS), and upload digital copies of detailed marks certificates (DMCs). No individual college seats are selected in Phase 1.
  • Phase 2 (Upcoming): Subject-wise vacant seats across all 36 Punjab districts will be published on the portal and physical notice boards of respective colleges. Candidates with completed profiles will submit hard copies of their application forms and attested testimonials directly to their target colleges to appear in interviews.

3
The 100-Mark Aggregate Formula (New Policy)

The total merit is computed out of 100 aggregate marks according to the updated policy breakdown:

Component Max Marks Details
Educational Qualification 85 Direct percentage based calculation for academic degrees with explicit flat/percent rules for MS/Ph.D.
Prior Performance as CTI 10 Awarded to candidates with prior performance during the 2025-2026 term.
Interview 05 Awarded based on presentation skills and subject mastery.

4
Terms of Service & Salary

Monthly Stipend: A flat stipend of Rs. 50,000/- per month is disbursed directly via crossed cheques or bank account transfers. No TA/DA is admissible.

Joining Period: Selected candidates must join duties within seven (07) days of the placement offer; otherwise, the offer stands withdrawn. Interns are allowed a maximum of 2 casual leaves per month.

Code of Conduct: Unapproved absence for more than 7 consecutive days, infliction of corporal punishment, or union/association activity results in immediate contract cancellation.

Frequently Asked Questions

اکثر پوچھے گئے سوالات

1. What is the maximum age limit for CTI?

Maximum 40 years (≤ 40) on the closing date of applications.

2. What is the new merit distribution?

Total 100 marks: 85 marks for educational qualifications, 10 marks for prior CTI performance, and 5 marks for interview.

3. What is the monthly stipend?

All selected CTIs receive a fixed stipend of Rs. 50,000/- per month.

4. Can I apply for college transfer after joining?

No. The placement is strictly college and subject specific. Transfers are not permitted.

5. Is a 3rd division degree acceptable?

No. You must have at least a 2nd division (45% marks) or 2.50 CGPA in the relevant subject.

6. How is the calculation system structured now?

The slab system has been completely replaced with a direct percentage-based system out of 85 marks.

7. How many leaves are allowed per month?

CTIs are entitled to a maximum of 2 casual leaves per month.

8. Will this job lead to permanent regular status?

No. Placement is purely temporary and stop-gap. It confers no right to regularization.

9. Are position holder marks included?

No, position holder marks are not part of the updated HED policy breakdown.

10. How to resolve marks discrepancies?

Submit a formal grievance to the Deputy Director (Colleges) within the designated complaint window.
