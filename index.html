Eligibility Criteria Summary

اہلیت کا معیار

Academic Requirements

Minimum 16 years of education (BS 4-Year or Master's) with at least 2nd Division (45%) or 2.50 CGPA. Graduate colleges require MS/M.Phil/PhD.

Age & Domicile

Maximum age limit is fixed at 40 years. Candidates must possess a valid Punjab Domicile. Both male and female candidates are eligible.

Stipend & Tenure

Selected interns will receive a fixed monthly stipend of Rs. 50,000/-. Tenure is temporary for the academic session.

The Guide to CTI Induction 2026–2027

سی ٹی آئی بھرتی کی مکمل تفصیلات اور قواعد

1
Introduction to the CTI Induction Scheme

The Higher Education Department (HED), Government of the Punjab, conducts the College Teaching Interns (CTI) program to resolve acute teaching staff shortages across government associate and graduate colleges in the province. The prime objective is to ensure that intermediate and undergraduate degree classes proceed without academic interruptions while regular PPSC recruitment processes are finalized. Hiring is strictly temporary as a stop-gap measure.

For the 2026–2027 session, the application framework uses a centralized digital portal (cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk) to manage profile registration and eliminate physical crowding. Placements are offered for an academic session or until regular faculty arrives, whichever occurs earlier.

2
Phase 1 & Phase 2 Application Procedure

درخواست دینے کا مرحلہ وار طریقہ کار

To maintain transparency, the induction is separated into two operational phases:

  • Phase 1 (Active Now - Profile Creation): Candidates must create an account on cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk using their CNIC. Applicants record personal details, academic marks (Matriculation through Master's/BS), and upload digital copies of detailed marks certificates (DMCs). No individual college seats are selected in Phase 1.
  • Phase 2 (Upcoming - Seat Applications & Interviews): Subject-wise vacant seats across all 36 Punjab districts will be published on the portal and physical notice boards of respective colleges. Candidates with completed profiles will submit hard copies of their application forms and attested testimonials directly to their target colleges to appear in interviews.

3
The 100-Mark Aggregate Formula

The total merit is computed out of 100 aggregate marks. Note: The 5 marks previously reserved for Board/University position holders have been completely removed from this policy.

Component Max Marks Details
Academic Qualification 85 Quantified according to official Level 1-5 slabs for Annual and Semester academic streams.
CTI Performance (2025–26) 05 Awarded to candidates with satisfactory performance during previous CTI service.
Local Catchment Candidate 05 Granted to applicants applying within their local tehsil/district area.
College Selection Interview 05 Awarded based on presentation skills, subject mastery, and communication.

4
Quantification Slabs Explained (Level 1 to 5)

Rather than arbitrary percentage math, the Higher Education Department uses a strict tier-based slab system. For example, in a General College with an MA/MSc degree (allocated 40 marks max):

  • Level 1 (90%+ Semester / 80%+ Annual): Full 40 Marks.
  • Level 2 (80%+ Semester / 70%+ Annual): 36 Marks.
  • Level 3 (70%+ Semester / 60%+ Annual): 32 Marks.
  • Level 4 (60%+ Semester / 50%+ Annual): 28 Marks.
  • Level 5 (50%+ Semester / 40%+ Annual): 24 Marks.

The same logic applies to Matric (15 marks), Intermediate (15 marks), and BS FYDP (55 marks) for General Colleges.

5
Terms of Service, Salary, & Code of Conduct

تنخواہ، مراعات اور سروس کے اصول

Monthly Stipend: A flat stipend of Rs. 50,000/- per month is disbursed directly via crossed cheques or bank account transfers. No TA/DA is admissible.

Joining Period: Selected candidates must join duties within seven (07) days of the placement offer; otherwise, the offer stands withdrawn and goes to the next-in-merit. Interns are allowed a maximum of 2 casual leaves per month.

Code of Conduct: Unapproved absence for more than 7 consecutive days, infliction of corporal punishment, or union/association activity results in immediate contract cancellation. Providing fake/bogus documents voids the contract ab initio and requires full stipend refund.

Frequently Asked Questions

اکثر پوچھے گئے سوالات (FAQs)

1. What is the maximum age limit for CTI?

The maximum age limit is 40 years, calculated against the closing date of the application period.

2. Are position holder marks included?

No. Position holder marks have been completely abolished in this policy.

3. What is the monthly stipend?

All selected CTIs receive a fixed stipend of Rs. 50,000/- per month.

4. Can I apply for college transfer after joining?

No. The placement is strictly college and subject specific. Transfers are not permitted.

5. Is a 3rd division degree acceptable?

No. You must have at least a 2nd division (45% marks) or 2.50 CGPA in the relevant subject.

6. How is CGPA converted to percentage?

If your DMC does not state percentage, conversion is done using the standard HEC conversion formula.

7. How many leaves are allowed per month?

CTIs are entitled to a maximum of 2 casual leaves per month.

8. Will this job lead to permanent regular status?

No. Placement is purely temporary and stop-gap. It confers no right to regularization.

9. What is Phase 1 on the portal?

Phase 1 is mandatory profile creation and document upload on cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk.

10. Where will vacant seats be announced?

In Phase 2, vacancies will be posted on the portal and physical college notice boards.

11. What is the joining deadline?

Selected candidates must join duty within 7 days of the placement offer.

12. Do candidates receive TA/DA for interviews?

No TA/DA is paid for attending interviews or joining duty.

13. What is the consequence of submitting forged documents?

Placement is cancelled ab initio, stipends must be refunded, and legal action is initiated.

14. What are interview marks based on?

The 5 marks are awarded based on satisfactory subject presentation and teaching skills.

15. What are the requirements for BS Colleges?

BS Colleges require MS/M.Phil/PhD. MA/MSc candidates are considered only if no higher degree candidate applies.

16. What is the Local Candidate criterion?

Applicants residing in the local tehsil/district catchment get 5 additional merit marks.

17. How are ties resolved in merit lists?

In the event of identical aggregate scores, the senior candidate in age is given priority.

18. Are marks awarded for previous CTI experience?

Yes. 5 marks are given for satisfactory service in the immediate 2025–26 session.

19. Are interns allowed to form associations or unions?

No. Union or association activities are strictly prohibited and result in immediate termination.

20. How to resolve marks discrepancies on tentative merit lists?

Submit a formal grievance to the Deputy Director (Colleges) within the designated complaint window.
