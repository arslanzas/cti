Eligibility Criteria Summary

اہلیت کا معیار

Academic Requirements

Minimum 16 years of education (Master's / BS FYDP) in the relevant subject with at least 2nd Division (45%) or 2.50 CGPA. Graduate colleges require MS/M.Phil/PhD[cite: 1].

Age & Domicile

The maximum age limit is fixed at 40 years. Candidates must possess a valid Punjab Domicile. Both male and female candidates are eligible[cite: 1].

Stipend & Tenure

Selected interns will receive a fixed stipend of Rs. 50,000/- per month. The tenure is temporary, lasting for the academic session or until a regular incumbent arrives[cite: 1].

The A to Z Guide to CTI Induction 2026-2027

سی ٹی آئی بھرتی کی مکمل تفصیلات

1
Introduction to the CTI Program

The Higher Education Department (HED), Government of the Punjab, initiates the College Teaching Interns (CTI) program to overcome the acute shortage of teaching staff in government associate and graduate colleges across the province. The primary objective is to ensure that students' academic sessions are not disrupted due to vacant regular faculty positions. The hiring is purely temporary, acting as a stop-gap arrangement[cite: 1].

For the academic year 2026-2027, the department has significantly revamped the application process, introducing a centralized online portal (`cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk`) to streamline profile creation and ensure transparency. Candidates are hired for a specific tenure—typically spanning one academic session—or until a regular incumbent (e.g., a PPSC selectee) arrives, whichever occurs earlier[cite: 1].

2
Phase 1 and Phase 2 Application Process

درخواست دینے کا مکمل طریقہ کار

To eradicate confusion and physical crowds at colleges, the 2026-2027 induction is split into two mandatory phases:

  • Phase 1 (Profile Creation): This is the digital onboarding phase. Candidates must visit the official portal, register using their CNIC, and build their comprehensive academic profile. This involves entering data from Matriculation up to Master's/M.Phil degrees, along with uploading scanned copies of DMCs (Detailed Marks Certificates), Domicile, and CNIC. No specific college choices are made in this phase.
  • Phase 2 (College Seat Selection): Once the HED concludes Phase 1, district-wise and college-wise vacant seats will be published. Candidates with completed profiles will then identify vacancies in their respective subjects. They will submit hard copies of their printed portal profiles, CVs, and attested documents to the designated colleges, where they will undergo interviews[cite: 1].

3
Detailed Breakdown of the 100-Mark Merit Formula

The merit calculation has been updated to ensure fairness. The total aggregate is calculated out of 100 marks[cite: 1]. Note: The 5 marks previously awarded to Board/University position holders have been completely abolished in this updated policy[cite: 1].

Merit Component Maximum Marks Description
Academic Qualification 85 Calculated based on HED's specific slab levels (Level 1 to 5) for both semester and annual systems across Matric, Inter, Graduation, and Master's[cite: 1].
Previous CTI Performance 05 Awarded exclusively to candidates who served satisfactorily as CTIs in the previous academic session (2025-2026).
Local Candidate 05 Granted to candidates applying in their local district/tehsil to encourage localized hiring and reduce transit issues.
Interview Marks 05 Awarded by the College Selection Committee based on subject knowledge, presentation skills, and confidence[cite: 1].

4
Understanding the Quantification Slabs (Level 1 to 5)

Instead of calculating a direct linear percentage, the HED utilizes a tier-based slab system[cite: 1]. For example, if you are applying to a General College with an MA/MSc degree (max 40 marks allocated for MA/MSc):

  • Level-1: 90%+ (Semester) / 80%+ (Annual) awards you the full 40 marks[cite: 1].
  • Level-2: 80%+ (Semester) / 70%+ (Annual) awards you 36 marks[cite: 1].
  • Level-3: 70%+ (Semester) / 60%+ (Annual) awards you 32 marks[cite: 1].
  • Level-4: 60%+ (Semester) / 50%+ (Annual) awards you 28 marks[cite: 1].
  • Level-5: 50%+ (Semester) / 40%+ (Annual) awards you 24 marks[cite: 1].

This same level-based logic applies to Matric (15 marks), Intermediate (15 marks), and BA/BSc (15 marks) for General colleges. For BS colleges, the distribution shifts to accommodate MS/M.Phil (20 marks) and Ph.D (25 marks)[cite: 1].

5
Terms of Employment, Salary, and Code of Conduct

تنخواہ اور نوکری کی شرائط

Stipend and Financials: The remuneration for CTIs across all levels (Intermediate or BS) is a flat stipend of Rs. 50,000 per month[cite: 1]. Disbursement is strictly managed through crossed cheques or direct bank account transfers[cite: 1]. Importantly, no TA/DA (Traveling Allowance / Daily Allowance) is admissible during the tenure[cite: 1].

Joining and Leaves: Upon issuance of the offer of placement, the candidate must report for duty within seven (07) days. Failure to do so results in the automatic withdrawal of the offer[cite: 1]. During their service, CTIs are entitled to a maximum of two (2) casual leaves per month[cite: 1].

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): CTIs are evaluated continuously by the College Principal. KPIs include good pedagogical skills, meticulous lesson planning, checking of students' homework, assisting the head teacher in curricular activities, and maintaining student progress reports[cite: 1].

Code of Conduct Violations: Misconduct, willful absence of more than 7 days continuously, inflicting corporal punishment, or forming any union/association will lead to immediate cancellation of the contract[cite: 1]. Furthermore, if a placement is secured using bogus or forged documents, the contract is void ab initio, and the candidate must refund all received stipends alongside facing legal action[cite: 1].

6
Final Merit Lists and Grievance Redressal

Transparency is a cornerstone of the CTI induction. Following the interviews, colleges are mandated to display tentative merit lists on their notice boards[cite: 1]. If a candidate finds a discrepancy in their marks computation, they are entitled to submit a formal complaint to the Deputy Director of Colleges within the stipulated timeframe[cite: 1]. A Grievance Redressal Committee, comprising senior faculty members and the Director of Education, is convened in each division to entertain and resolve these objections before the final merit list is uploaded and displayed[cite: 1].

Upon successful completion of the tenure, candidates are awarded an Experience Certificate by the Higher Education Department, which is highly valuable for future regular PPSC lecturer applications[cite: 1].

Frequently Asked Questions

اکثر پوچھے گئے سوالات (FAQs)

1. What is the maximum age limit for CTI?

The maximum age limit is 40 years. This is calculated against the closing date of the application period[cite: 1].

2. Are position holder marks included?

No. In the updated 2026-2027 policy, the 5 marks previously given for university or board positions have been abolished[cite: 1].

3. What is the stipend amount?

All CTIs will receive a fixed monthly stipend of Rs. 50,000/-[cite: 1].

4. Can I apply for transfer after joining?

No. Placement is strictly subject and college specific. Transfers are not permitted under any circumstances[cite: 1].

5. Is a 3rd division degree acceptable?

No. The minimum requirement is a 2nd Division (at least 45% marks) or a 2.50 CGPA in the relevant subject[cite: 1].

6. What happens if my DMC only has a CGPA?

If your DMC does not explicitly mention a percentage, your CGPA will be converted into a percentage using the official HEC conversion formula[cite: 1].

7. How many leaves are allowed?

CTIs are permitted a maximum of two (02) casual leaves per month during their contract period[cite: 1].

8. Will this job be regularized?

No. The placement confers absolutely no right to regular employment. It is purely a temporary, stop-gap arrangement[cite: 1].

9. What is Phase 1?

Phase 1 is strictly for profile creation on the HED online portal. You must upload your personal data and educational documents to become eligible for college applications[cite: 1].

10. Where will I see the vacant seats?

In Phase 2, vacant seat details will be published on the official online portal as well as on the physical notice boards of the respective colleges[cite: 1].

11. What is the joining time limit?

You must join duty within 7 days of receiving the offer letter. Otherwise, the next candidate on the merit list will be called[cite: 1].

12. Do I get TA/DA for the interview?

No. Traveling Allowance and Daily Allowance (TA/DA) are not admissible for attending interviews or joining the post[cite: 1].

13. What if I provide a fake document?

If caught, your placement will be voided ab initio, you will have to refund all stipends, and legal action will be initiated against you[cite: 1].

14. What are the Interview Marks based on?

The 5 interview marks are awarded based on a satisfactory presentation on the subject, assessing pedagogical skills and knowledge[cite: 1].

15. What are the requirements for BS Colleges?

BS Colleges primarily require an MS, M.Phil, or Ph.D degree. An MA/MSc candidate will only be considered if no higher degree candidate applies[cite: 1].

16. What does "Local Candidate" mean?

Candidates applying to colleges situated within their local tehsil or district catchment receive 5 additional merit marks.

17. How is merit resolved in case of a tie?

If two candidates have the identical aggregate score, the older candidate usually receives priority, subject to the final committee's decision.

18. Are there marks for past CTI experience?

Yes. If you served as a CTI in the immediate previous session (2025-2026) with satisfactory performance, you get 5 marks.

19. Can I form a union?

No. CTIs have no right to form unions or associations. Indulging in such activities will result in automatic contract cancellation[cite: 1].

20. Who do I contact if my marks are calculated wrong on the list?

You must submit a grievance to the Deputy Director (Colleges) within the designated complaint dates before the final list is issued[cite: 1].
